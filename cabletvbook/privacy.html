Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Developer: Nivid • Official App Policy
Our Privacy Pledge to Cable Operators Cable TV Book is designed specifically for local cable television & broadband operators. All subscriber ledgers, contact numbers, and billing collection entries input into your account remain strictly confidential. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your subscriber records with any third-party advertisers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cable TV Book, developed and operated by Nivid ("we", "our", or "us"). Cable TV Book is a dedicated business management application built for local cable television operators, network managers, and local broadband administrators ("Operators", "you") to manage subscriber rosters, coverage sectors/areas, subscription plans, and monthly billing collections.

We respect your privacy and the sensitive nature of your customer ledgers. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is stored and protected, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

A. Operator Account Data

  • Operator Full Name: For account identification.
  • Enterprise / Network Name: Displayed on receipts and app headers.
  • Email Address: For authentication, password resets, and critical updates.
  • Phone Number: For secure OTP verification.
  • Network Areas & Packages: Custom zones and subscription rate cards created by you.

B. Subscriber Records (Entered by Operator)

  • Subscriber name, door/installation address, and contact number.
  • Set-Top Box (STB) identifier / Hardware serial number.
  • Assigned area/sub-network and active monthly package.
  • Payment ledger history (Paid, Due, Partial) and date stamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for operational and business management purposes:

  • Authentication & Security: Authenticating operator accounts securely through Firebase Phone Authentication (SMS OTP) and Email/Password.
  • Core Business Operations: Calculating monthly dues, generating area-wise collection totals, and syncing offline ledger entries to the cloud.
  • Data Backup & Recovery: Providing real-time cloud backup via Google Cloud Firestore to safeguard against mobile device damage or loss.
  • Customer Support: Providing troubleshooting assistance when you contact us for technical support.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

We implement rigorous technical and organizational measures to protect your commercial data:

Encrypted In-Transit

All data transfers between the mobile app and cloud servers use modern TLS/HTTPS encryption protocols.

Encrypted At-Rest

Google Cloud Firestore automatically encrypts all database records and backups at rest.

Tenant Isolation

Firestore Security Rules strictly isolate data so operators can only read or modify their own subscribers.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

We partner with leading cloud infrastructure providers to guarantee high uptime, reliability, and security:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions)
  • Google Cloud Platform (Hosting, distributed databases, automated backups)
  • Google Play Services (App distribution, auto-updates, and crash telemetry)

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play & App Store Compliance)

In strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policy and Apple App Store Review Guidelines, you have full sovereignty over your data and account lifecycle:

In-App Immediate Account & Data Purge

You can permanently delete your operator profile, all subscriber registers, payment collections, and billing history at any time directly within the application: Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account

Upon deletion, your operator account credentials are wiped immediately from Firebase Auth, and all linked Firestore documents are permanently purged.

Alternatively, you may request manual deletion of your account and records by emailing our team at srinivasasoftwares@gmail.com with your registered phone number.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our features or regulatory changes. In the event of material updates, we will notify operators via in-app banner or require acknowledgement upon opening the app. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of any revised terms.
