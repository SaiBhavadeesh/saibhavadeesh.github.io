Privacy Policy for Foody Cookbook

Effective Date: October 2026 • Developer: Nivid

1. Overview

Foody Cookbook is a personal recipe and cooking companion application developed by Nivid. We respect your privacy and design our applications to minimize personal data collection.

2. Data Collection & Use

Foody Cookbook allows users to save favourite recipes, create custom notes, and organize meals. User-generated bookmarks and preferences are saved locally on your device or linked securely to your authenticated account. We do not sell or share personal culinary data with third parties.
